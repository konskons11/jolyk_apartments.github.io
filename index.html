Locale

The Heart of Skala Marion

Skala Marion sits on the southwest coast of Thassos, nestled between three natural bays. Once the coastal harbour of the mountain village Maries, it grew around the loading docks where iron ore from inland mines was shipped out. Today, it retains that unhurried, working-village soul that so many Aegean resorts have traded away.

Three beaches await within walking distance:
· Skala Marion Beach — shallow, family-perfect waters directly in front of the village,
· Atspas Bay — a turquoise cove fringed by trees, beloved by those in the know, and
· Platanes Beach — a long open bay of fine sand with room to breathe.

JoLyk Apartments is flawlessly situated just 100 meters from the village center and its central beach.
Feel free to leave your car in our complimentary private parking and explore on foot.

Discover Thassos

Mountain · 12 km

Maries & the Lake

Built in a dramatic gorge at high altitude, Maries is one of the oldest mountain villages of Thassos featuring a tranquil lake and legendary local cuisine — slow-cooked goat, wild honey, and handmade spoon sweets of every fruit the mountain offers.

History · 31 km

Limenas (Thassos Town)

The island's capital brims with ancient wonders: the Archaeological Museum, the Ancient Agora, and the remarkably preserved Ancient Theatre.

Nightlife · 8–12 km

Limenaria & Potos (Nightlife)

Thassos's second town, Limenaria, has a lively waterfront and the historic Palataki. Just south, Potos is the island's nightlife hub — beach bars, cocktail lounges, and live music last into the small hours.

Nature · Island-Wide

Nature Trails

Thassos is blanketed in pine and chestnut forest. The ~100 km perimeter road reveals a new landscape every kilometre — from Byzantine monasteries and Ypsarion summit (1,200m) to marble beaches and hidden coves.
